One player, more of your music

A listening space
that moves with you.

Made to bring playback, playlists, and the little details of listening into one native Android experience.

01 / PLAY

Keep the music moving.

Search and play from the YouTube Music catalog, with queue controls, background playback, and media controls on your lock screen.

02 / ORGANIZE

Your library, in rhythm.

Create, sort, pin, and edit local playlists. Bring in public Spotify and Apple Music playlists by pasting a link.

03 / TAKE IT WITH YOU

Listen beyond the signal.

Save tracks for offline playback, follow synchronized lyrics from LRCLIB, and optionally scrobble listening activity to Last.fm.

Service names are used only to describe compatibility. Rushtify is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by those services.
